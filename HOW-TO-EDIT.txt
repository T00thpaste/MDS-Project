MDS PROJECT WEBSITE: GUIDE FOR TEAMS
====================================

Your team has one folder inside "teams" (for example teams/team05).
Only edit files inside YOUR folder. Do not touch "assets" or other teams' folders.

Inside your folder:
  index.html    Project tab: your topic and problem statement (this is your first page)
  results.html  Results tab: graphs and explanations
  team.html     Team tab: member photos and short bios
  info.js       Your tile on the main page: change the team name and project title in it
  images/       Put your graphs and photos here

How to edit
  1. Open the .html file in any text editor (VS Code is best).
  2. Look for the comments that start with "EDIT" or "HOW TO". They tell you what to change.
  3. Replace the placeholder text with your own text.
  4. Save, then double-click the file to open it in your browser and check it.

Adding a graph or a photo
  1. Copy the image into your "images" folder (use simple names like graph1.png, riya.jpg).
  2. In the HTML, change the image line, for example:
       src="../../assets/placeholder.svg"   ->   src="images/graph1.png"
  3. Write a short sentence in alt="..." that describes the image.

Team photo or logo
  Save it as images/team.jpg (png or webp also work) in your folder. It replaces the
  placeholder on your Team page and replaces the chart on your tile on the main page.
  Member photos are shown as circles, so use square photos with the face in the middle.

Adding more graphs or members
  Copy everything between <!-- BEGIN RESULT --> and <!-- END RESULT -->
  (or BEGIN MEMBER and END MEMBER) and paste it right below. Then edit the copy.

Team name
  The name at the top left ("Team 5") is in all three HTML files. Change it in each one.

Do not move or rename files or folders. The links between pages depend on them.

FOR THE ORGANISER
  - You do not edit the main page. Each team sets its own tile text in its info.js
    and its tile picture by saving images/team.jpg.
  - Hosting: all links are relative, so the folder works on GitHub Pages, a college server,
    or just opened from a drive. Keep the folder structure as it is.
  - _generator/generate.py recreates the team folders. Do not run it with --force after
    teams have started editing, or their work will be overwritten.
